# Story Bank

*Whose stories these are: [NAME]*

The stories behind the person's results, in their own words. Interview prep keeps turning up results that never made it onto the resume, and those are often the strongest bullets. Claude adds new facts here as they come up, and new bullets get written from them.

How to write one:

- One entry per story: the situation, what the person did, and the result with its number
- Tag it with what it proves, using the same tags as Bullet Bank.md, like `[onboarding]` `[budget]` `[training]`
- Write down whether the work caused the result or the result just came after it. A bullet only says "by" when the person says the work caused it
- Say whether it's on the resume yet
